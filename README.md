3D Printed Infinite Notebook (A6)
A fully printable, customizable notebook system inspired by Gazzaladra's design. Uses standard 1.75mm filament as hinge pins and regular rubber bands to bind paper signatures.

Features
A6 Paper Size: Fits folded A5 paper sheets inside.

No Supports Needed: Prints flat on your bed with 0.4mm nozzle settings.

Filament Hinges: Just slide in a piece of 1.75mm PLA/PETG filament for the spine hinge pins.

Rubber Band Binding: Recessed inner slots designed for standard 1.4mm x 1.4mm rubber bands (like #32 or #33 elastics).

Pen Slot: Holds pens around 8.5mm to 10.5mm wide.

Recommended Print Settings
Layer Height: 0.2mm

Wall Loops / Perimeters: 3 to 4 (for strong hinge loops)

Infill: 15% - 20% (Gyroid or Grid works fine)

Material: PLA or PETG

Assembly Quick Steps
Line up the front and back cover hinge loops with the center spine.

Push a straight piece of 1.75mm filament down each hinge hole. Trim the ends flush.

Thread rubber bands through the internal spine hooks.

Fold your A5 paper in half, slip them under the bands, and you're good to go!
